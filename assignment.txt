a chat between Rachel (who is an agent [real person] of Libra car insurance company in Israel) and a client, 
Rachel is trying to make the client renew their insurance which is about to expire.

agent: "Hi! This is Rachel from Libra. Your car insurance will expire this month. 
The price for a new insurance is 7000 NIS including discount. Can we renew it?"
client: "Hi thanks. I want a better offer before i decide."

client: "Is this the best you can do?"
agent: "Did you get a better offer elsewhere?"
client: "Checking."
agent: "OK, waiting :)"
client: "How much is it for 2 drivers over 50 years old? And do you have insurance for a casual (random) driver?"
agent: "Checking... there: for a casual driver it is 35 NIS per week if they are above 24 years old, 
and 30 NIS per day for younger driver"
client: "Thanks. I decided to go with another offer. I hope you'll be more atractive next year. 
No need to try to convince me further."
agent: "What is the offer you got? Maybe I can improve mine."
client: "Stop disturbing me."
